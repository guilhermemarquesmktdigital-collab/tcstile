The part you never see

What holds up a shower isn't the tile. It's what's under it.

Before a single tile goes on the wall, this shower gets a fully bonded waterproofing membrane, correctly sloped mud bed, and sealed seams at every corner and penetration. It's the least photogenic part of the job — and the reason a shower lasts twenty years instead of five.

  • Fully waterproofed pans, walls, and niches on every wet area
  • Correctly sloped shower floors so water actually drains
  • Sealed seams and penetrations — no shortcuts behind the tile

About TCS Tile Company

Meet Fernando.

For more than 30 years, Fernando has made his living down on his knees on job sites — setting tile, leveling floors, and building showers that are still watertight decades later. He founded TCS Tile Company Solution LLC to do things the way he always has: no subcontracted crews, no cut corners, just one experienced tradesman who shows up and does the work himself.

From the first chalk line to the last wipe of the grout haze, every TCS project is planned and installed by Fernando personally — which means what you agree to at the estimate is what shows up on install day.
